# NAG Section A (Adult) — Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs)

Source: MOH Malaysia National Antimicrobial Guideline (NAG),
https://sites.google.com/moh.gov.my/nag/contents/section-a-adult/a13-sexually-transmitted-infections
Transcribed from text supplied by the project owner. Reference data only — not yet
wired into `src/lib/nagPathways.ts`. Numbering follows the source (it has no 2.1.3).

STIs with similar presentations are managed by syndrome: anogenital ulcer disease (syphilis, HSV,
chancroid, LGV, donovanosis, Mpox); urethral discharge (gonorrhoea, chlamydia, NGU,
epididymo-orchitis); vaginal discharge (trichomoniasis, BV, candidiasis, cervicitis); anorectal
discharge (gonorrhoea, chlamydia, LGV). The syndromic approach ensures prompt, effective treatment.

Cross-references: Appendix 3 (penicillin desensitisation), Ocular Infections section (gonococcal
conjunctivitis), Mpox Management Guidelines (MOH).

## 1. Anogenital Ulcer Disease

### 1.1 Syphilis (Treponema pallidum)

#### 1.1.1 Primary, secondary, early latent (infection within last 2 years)
- **Preferred:** Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM STAT; OR Procaine penicillin 600,000 units IM q24h for 10 days
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy):** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 14 days
- **Comments:** Examine, investigate and treat partner(s) epidemiologically. Abstain from sex 1 week
  after patient and partners complete treatment. If treatment is interrupted ≥1 day, the course may
  need restarting. Warn of Jarisch-Herxheimer reaction and anaphylaxis/allergy.

#### 1.1.2 Late latent, gumma (benign tertiary), cardiovascular
- **Preferred:** Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM weekly for 3 weeks (Day 1, 8, 15); OR Procaine
  penicillin 600,000 units IM q24h for 14 days
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy):** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 28 days
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above. Cardiovascular syphilis: consider
  prednisolone 40–60mg PO q24h for 3 days starting 24 hours before antibiotics. If weekly benzathine
  doses are interrupted by ≥2 weeks, restart the entire course.

#### 1.1.3 Neurosyphilis
- **Preferred:** Benzylpenicillin 4MU IV q4h for 14 days; OR Procaine penicillin 2.4MU IM q24h for
  14 days PLUS \*Probenecid 500mg PO q6h for 14 days
- **Alternative:** Penicillin allergy without anaphylaxis: Ceftriaxone 2g IM or IV q24h for 14 days.
  Anaphylaxis to penicillin: Doxycycline 200mg PO q12h for 28 days.
- **Comments:** Consider prednisolone 40–60mg PO q24h for 3 days starting 24 hours before
  antibiotics. \*Indication not listed in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 1.1.4 Syphilis in HIV
Treat as appropriate for stage of infection.

#### 1.1.5 Incubating syphilis / epidemiological treatment
- **Preferred:** Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM STAT
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy):** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 14 days

### 1.2 Syphilis in Pregnancy

#### 1.2.1 Primary, secondary, early latent
- **Preferred:** Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM STAT; OR Procaine penicillin 600,000 units IM q24h for 10 days
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy):** \*Desensitise and treat with penicillin (no proven alternatives).
  If desensitisation fails: Ceftriaxone 500mg IM q24h for 10 days; OR Azithromycin 2g PO STAT; OR
  Erythromycin ethylsuccinate 800mg PO q6h for 14 days.
- **Comments:** Partner management. If a macrolide is used, assess and treat the neonate at birth.
  \*Benefit of treatment outweighs the risk of allergic reaction in skin test and desensitisation;
  see Appendix 3 for the protocol.

#### 1.2.2 Late latent, gumma, cardiovascular
- **Preferred:** Benzathine penicillin 2.4MU IM weekly for 3 weeks (Day 1, 8, 15); OR Procaine
  penicillin 600,000 units IM q24h for 14 days
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy):** Erythromycin ethylsuccinate 800mg PO q6h for 28 days
- **Comments:** Partner management; neonate assessment if macrolide used. Cardiovascular syphilis:
  consider prednisolone 40–60mg PO q24h for 3 days starting 24 hours before antibiotics.

#### 1.2.3 Neurosyphilis
- **Preferred:** Benzylpenicillin 4MU IV q4h for 14 days; OR Procaine penicillin 2.4MU IM q24h for
  14 days PLUS \*Probenecid 500mg PO q6h for 14 days
- **Alternative (penicillin allergy without anaphylaxis):** Ceftriaxone 2g IM or IV q24h for 14 days
- **Comments:** Consider prednisolone 40–60mg PO q24h for 3 days starting 24 hours before
  antibiotics. \*Indication not listed in MOH Drug Formulary.

### 1.3 Genital Herpes (HSV-1 and -2)

#### 1.3.1 First episode
- **Preferred:** Acyclovir 400mg PO q8h for 7–10 days
- **Alternative:** \*Valacyclovir 1g PO q12h for 7–10 days
- **Comments:** Supportive measures (saline bathing, analgesia, local anaesthetics). Oral antivirals
  within 5 days of onset and while new lesions are forming. Topical antivirals are less effective,
  not recommended (association with acyclovir-resistant strains), and add no benefit to oral
  treatment. \*Not in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 1.3.2 Recurrent episode
- **Preferred:** Short course: Acyclovir 800mg PO q8h for 2 days; 5-day course: Acyclovir 800mg PO q12h for 5 days
- **Alternative:** Short course: \*Valacyclovir 500mg PO q12h for 3 days; 5-day course: \*Valacyclovir 1g PO q24h for 5 days
- **Comments:** \*Not in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 1.3.3 Suppressive therapy
For ≥6 recurrences/year, severe or prolonged episodes, or psychosocial problems.
- **Preferred:** Acyclovir 400mg PO q12h for up to 1 year, then reassess. Breakthrough recurrences:
  increase to Acyclovir 400mg PO q8h for 7–10 days.
- **Alternative:** \*Valacyclovir 500mg PO q24h for up to 1 year, then reassess. If ≥10
  recurrences/year: \*Valacyclovir 1g PO q24h for up to 1 year, then reassess.
- **Comments:** \*Not in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 1.3.4 Severe disease (requiring hospitalisation)
- **Preferred:** Acyclovir 5–10mg/kg/dose IV q8h for 10–14 days

#### 1.3.5 First episode in pregnancy
- **Preferred:** Acyclovir 400mg PO q8h for 7–10 days; for third-trimester acquisition, continue till delivery
- **Alternative:** \*Valacyclovir 500mg PO q12h for 7–10 days
- **Comments:** \*Not in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 1.3.6 Recurrent episode in pregnancy
- **Preferred:** Acyclovir 400mg PO q8h, starting at 36 weeks' gestation
- **Alternative:** \*Valacyclovir 500mg PO q12h, starting at 36 weeks' gestation
- **Comments:** \*Not in MOH Drug Formulary.

### 1.4 Other Anogenital Ulcer Diseases

#### 1.4.1 Chancroid (Haemophilus ducreyi)
- **Preferred:** Azithromycin 1g PO STAT
- **Alternative:** Ceftriaxone 250mg IM STAT; OR Ciprofloxacin 500mg PO q12h for 3 days; OR
  Erythromycin ethylsuccinate 800mg PO q8h for 7 days
- **Comments:** Examine and treat partners from the 10 days before symptom onset even if
  asymptomatic. Re-examine 3–7 days after starting therapy; success = symptomatic improvement
  within 3 days and substantial re-epithelialisation within 7 days. In pregnancy/breastfeeding use
  azithromycin, ceftriaxone or erythromycin.

#### 1.4.2 Lymphogranuloma venereum (Chlamydia trachomatis L1–3)
- **Preferred:** \*Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 21 days
- **Alternative:** Azithromycin 1g PO weekly for 3 weeks; OR Erythromycin ethylsuccinate 800mg PO q6h for 21 days
- **Comments:** Partners within 60 days: evaluate and treat if symptomatic; if asymptomatic,
  empirical Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 7 days. Proctitis/anorectal discharge common, especially
  in MSM. Aspirate fluctuant buboes through healthy adjacent skin; surgical incision is
  contraindicated. With an alternative regimen or in pregnancy, consider test-of-cure 4 weeks after
  treatment. \*Doxycycline is contraindicated in pregnancy.

#### 1.4.3 Granuloma inguinale / donovanosis (Klebsiella granulomatis)
- **Preferred:** Azithromycin 1g PO weekly or 500mg q24h
- **Alternative:** \*Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h; OR Trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole 160/800mg PO q12h;
  OR Erythromycin ethylsuccinate 800mg PO q6h
- **Comments:** Screen and treat partners within 60 days before symptom onset. Treat at least
  3 weeks or until lesions heal completely. \*Doxycycline is contraindicated in pregnancy.

### 1.5 Mpox
Refer to MOH Guidelines on Mpox Management in Malaysia.

## 2. Urethral Discharge

### 2.1 Gonorrhoea (Neisseria gonorrhoeae)

#### 2.1.1 Uncomplicated (cervix, urethra, rectum, pharynx)
- **Preferred**
  - Cervix, urethra, rectum: Ceftriaxone 500mg IM STAT (BW >150kg: 1g IM STAT) PLUS
    \*Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 7 days (if chlamydia not excluded)
  - Pharynx: same regimen
- **Alternative**
  - Cervix, urethra, rectum with cephalosporin allergy: Gentamicin 240mg IM STAT PLUS Azithromycin 2g PO STAT
  - Pharynx with anaphylaxis or severe cephalosporin reaction: consult for expert opinion
- **Comments:** Examine, investigate and treat partners within 60 days irrespective of test results.
  Abstain from sex for 1 week after treatment completion and symptom resolution.
  \*Doxycycline is contraindicated in pregnancy.

#### 2.1.2 In pregnancy
- **Preferred:** Ceftriaxone 500mg IM STAT (BW >150kg: 1g IM STAT) PLUS Azithromycin 1g PO STAT (if chlamydia not excluded)
- **Alternative:** Anaphylaxis or severe cephalosporin reaction: consult for expert opinion
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above.

#### 2.1.4 Disseminated gonococcal infection (DGI)
- **Preferred**
  - Arthritis-dermatitis syndrome: Ceftriaxone 1g IV/IM q24h for 7 days
  - Purulent arthritis: Ceftriaxone 1g IV/IM q24h for 7–14 days
  - Meningitis: Ceftriaxone 1–2g IV q12h–24h for 10–14 days
  - Endocarditis: Ceftriaxone 1–2g IV q12h–24h for 4–6 weeks
- **Alternative:** Arthritis-dermatitis syndrome: Cefotaxime 1g IV q8h for 7 days
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above.

#### 2.1.5 Conjunctivitis
Refer to the Ocular Infections section.

### 2.2 Chlamydia (Chlamydia trachomatis)

#### 2.2.1 Uncomplicated (cervix, urethra, rectum, pharynx)
- **Preferred:** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 7 days
- **Alternative:** Azithromycin 1g PO STAT; OR \*Levofloxacin 500mg PO q24h for 7 days
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above. \*Indication not in MOH Drug Formulary.

#### 2.2.2 In pregnancy
- **Preferred:** Azithromycin 1g PO STAT
- **Alternative:** Amoxicillin 500mg PO q8h for 7 days

### 2.3 Non-gonococcal urethritis (NGU)
**Organisms:** C. trachomatis; Mycoplasma genitalium; Ureaplasma spp.; Trichomonas vaginalis;
adenoviruses; HSV.

#### 2.3.1 First episode
- **Preferred:** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 7 days
- **Alternative:** Azithromycin 500mg PO STAT, then 250mg q24h for 4 days
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above; consider empiric chlamydia treatment for partners.

#### 2.3.2 Recurrent and persistent
- **Preferred**
  - If first-line was doxycycline: Azithromycin 500mg PO STAT, then 250mg PO q24h for 4 days PLUS
    Metronidazole 400mg PO q12h for 5 days
  - If first-line was azithromycin: \*Moxifloxacin 400mg PO q24h for 10–14 days PLUS Metronidazole
    400mg PO q24h for 5 days *(dosing frequency as printed in the source; differs from q12h above)*
- **Comments:** Partner management and abstinence as above; consider empiric chlamydia treatment for
  partners. \*Indication not in MOH Drug Formulary.

### 2.4 Epididymo-orchitis / epididymitis

#### 2.4.1 STI-related
- **Preferred:** Ceftriaxone 500mg IM STAT PLUS Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 10 days

#### 2.4.2 STI-related, gonorrhoea unlikely
- **Preferred:** Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 10 days

**Comments (both):** Abstain from sex until patient and partners complete treatment and symptoms
resolve. Review the diagnosis if no improvement after 3 days; review at 2 weeks for compliance,
partner notification and resolution. Watch for complications: hydrocoele, abscess, infarction, infertility.

## 3. Vaginal Discharge

### 3.1 Trichomoniasis (Trichomonas vaginalis)
- **Preferred:** Metronidazole 400mg PO q12h for 7 days; OR Metronidazole 2g PO STAT\*
- **Comments:** Treat partners simultaneously. Abstain from sex for 1 week after treatment and
  symptom resolution. \*Single high-dose metronidazole has more GI side effects and a higher failure
  rate, especially if partners are not treated concurrently.

### 3.2 Cervicitis
- **Preferred:** \*Doxycycline 100mg PO q12h for 7 days
- **Alternative:** Azithromycin 1g PO STAT
- **Comments:** Presumptive treatment of chlamydia. \*Doxycycline is contraindicated in pregnancy.

### 3.3 Bacterial vaginosis
**Organisms:** anaerobes (Prevotella, Mobiluncus, Gardnerella vaginalis, Mycoplasma hominis).

- **Preferred:** Metronidazole 400mg PO q12h for 7 days; OR Metronidazole 2g PO STAT\*
- **Alternative:** Clindamycin 300mg PO q12h for 7 days
- **Comments:** Not an STI but often found during STI screening. \*Single high-dose metronidazole
  has more GI side effects.

### 3.4 Vulvovaginal candidiasis (Candida spp.)

#### 3.4.1 Uncomplicated
- **Preferred:** Clotrimazole 500mg single vaginal pessary STAT
- **Alternative:** Fluconazole 150–200mg PO STAT

#### 3.4.2 Complicated (severe vaginitis symptoms)
- **Preferred:** Fluconazole 150–200mg PO q72h for 2 doses (Day 1 and 4)
- **Alternative:** Clotrimazole 500mg vaginal pessary q72h for 2 doses (Day 1 and 4)

#### 3.4.3 Recurrent (≥3 symptomatic episodes in <1 year)
- **Preferred:** Fluconazole 150–200mg PO q72h for 3 doses (Day 1, 4, 7), then weekly for 6 months
- **Alternative:** Clotrimazole 500mg vaginal pessary weekly for 6 months
- **Comments:** Treat each episode with a longer course of topical azole.

#### 3.4.4 In pregnancy
- **Preferred:** Clotrimazole 500mg single vaginal pessary STAT
- **Alternative:** Topical azole for at least 1 week
- **Comments:** Oral azoles are contraindicated in pregnancy.
